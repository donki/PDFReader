## Description

```
PDF Editor reads, annotates and signs your PDFs without a single document leaving your computer.
No accounts, no ads, no connection: everything happens on your PC.

READ
• A library of your recent documents: pick up where you left off.
• Whole page or fit to width, zoom with the wheel or the buttons, jump to a page.
• Opens password-protected PDFs (the password is never stored).
• "Open with" from File Explorer.

ANNOTATE AND SIGN
• Pen, highlighter, rectangles and ellipses.
• Text boxes: alignment, vertical text, four typefaces (Open Sans, Lora, Roboto Mono and the
  handwritten Caveat), size, bold and colour. Move, resize and edit them.
• Cover: a white box over anything you want hidden; write on top to correct a value.
• Handwritten signature with the mouse or the touch screen; saved and reused.
• Everything is saved flattened into a new PDF that any viewer shows. The original is never touched.

TOOLS
• Merge several PDFs in the order you choose.
• Split by page ranges or one page per document.
• Organize pages with thumbnails: rotate, reorder, delete or extract.
• Images to PDF (JPG, PNG) and PDF to images (PNG in a ZIP).
• Protect with a password, or remove it.
• Page numbers and watermark.
• Save the result wherever you want with "Save as".

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PC
No server, no account, no analytics, no ads. Your documents stay on your computer.

Free software under the MIT license. In English and Spanish, light and dark. The same app exists
for Android.
```
